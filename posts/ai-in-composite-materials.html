What Can AI Do for Composite Materials?

Changing the winding angle of a carbon fiber layer changes how a pressure vessel carries load. Which angles deserve a closer look? In our 2025 review, we explored three ways AI can help engineers make decisions like this.

Prediction. In our pressure vessel study, we trained a model on inexpensive analytical calculations, then refined it with a smaller set of finite element simulations. That let us explore how winding angle and layer thickness affect the vessel’s response before choosing which designs to simulate in detail.

Generation. A model can propose new fiber arrangements based on micrographs. To use them as virtual specimens, we need to check more than appearance. For example, if we want to study resin flow, the gaps between neighboring fibers matter.

Automation. Imagine a camera spotting a misplaced fiber strip during layup. Detection gives the operator information. A system that uses the signal to adjust the next pass and checks the result closes the feedback loop.

Across all three approaches, I’d start with one question: what decision will change because of the model’s output?
