Samsung Electronics · September 2024–Present

HBM · DRAM Structural Mechanics & Advanced Simulation

How does a warped wafer affect a bond between layers? At Samsung Electronics, I use finite element simulations to connect deformation across memory structures with stress at individual interfaces.

From wafer warpage to local stress

I model deformation at wafer, chip, and package levels. I then use the displacement field from a larger model to study stress in a smaller region, such as a bonded interface.

Bonding and fracture

When dissimilar materials are bonded, I examine how stress develops at their interface and how an existing crack affects mechanical reliability. These analyses help identify where a design change could reduce the risk of failure.

AI-assisted prediction

I use finite element results to train models that predict structural behavior. Alongside simulation automation, this helps me evaluate design changes more efficiently.
