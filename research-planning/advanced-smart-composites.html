Wearable robotics · Multifunctional composites

Advanced Smart Composites

I studied how a composite ankle spring for an exoskeleton can do more than support a load. In one study, I used changes in the spring’s capacitance as it bent to estimate ground reaction forces with a neural network. In a companion study, I investigated how the motion of a composite spring could generate electricity through the triboelectric effect.
