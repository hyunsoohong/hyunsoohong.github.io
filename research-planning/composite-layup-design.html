Composite laminates · Image analysis

Deep Learning for Composite Layup Design

The fiber angles in a finished composite part can differ from the intended layup, changing how the part responds to load. I used a convolutional neural network to estimate ply angles from images of laminate cross sections. The estimates provide a way to compare the manufactured part with its design.
