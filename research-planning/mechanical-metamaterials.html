Mechanical metamaterials · Structural optimization

AI-Driven Mechanical Metamaterials

I use machine learning to design structures that support a load while isolating low-frequency vibrations. For buckling and kirigami structures, I optimized the cell geometry to achieve a quasi-zero-stiffness response and tested the 3D-printed kirigami design under compression and vibration. In a related collaboration, we designed a resonant truss whose vibration attenuation changes when water is added.
