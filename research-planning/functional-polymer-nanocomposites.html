Polymer films · Molecular dynamics

Functional Polymer Nanocomposites

I studied how microwave treatment during solvent evaporation affects the crystal structure of PVDF films containing titanium dioxide nanoparticles. I paired experiments on the films with molecular dynamics simulations to investigate how the polymer chains change conformation. In related collaborative work, we studied how bovine serum albumin affects the adhesion of copper electrodes to PVDF films.
