Marine structures · Composite design

Composite Supports for LNG Systems

On an LNG vessel, supports between the inner and outer pipes must carry the weight of the inner pipe and limit vibration. I studied a support made from carbon- and glass-fiber composites, using finite element analysis to examine its structural response. The project also addressed fatigue under repeated loading.
