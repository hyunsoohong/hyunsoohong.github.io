AI for Composite Pressure Vessels

I used deep transfer learning to predict how filament-wound composite pressure vessels deform under load. I first trained a model on analytical solutions, then fine-tuned it on a smaller set of finite element simulations. With this model, I explored how winding angle and layer thickness affect vessel behavior and used those predictions to guide design optimization.
