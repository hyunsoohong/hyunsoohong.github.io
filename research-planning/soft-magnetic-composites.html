Magnetic materials · Electric motors

Soft Magnetic Composites for Electric Motors

We coated iron particles with an insulating thermoplastic, PEEK, and pressed them into lightweight magnetic cores for brushless DC motors. In prototype tests, motors with the composite cores showed lower core losses and higher efficiency than the laminated-core motor used for comparison. I contributed to the research methodology and provided resources for the study.
